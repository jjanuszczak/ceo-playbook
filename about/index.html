Operator-Advisor for Fintech, Technology, and Energy Ventures in Southeast Asia
#

I help boards, CEOs, investors, and corporate ventures make high-stakes decisions about market entry, regulation, venture building, and capital allocation. My work turns an attractive idea into an operating plan that can survive local regulation, commercial reality, and the hard work of execution.

I am not a management consultancy. I am a former operator who has carried P&L responsibility, built teams, managed investment decisions, and dealt directly with the consequences when a strategy meets the market.

What I Do
#

Southeast Asia market entry and strategic structuring
#

I help technology, fintech, and energy businesses assess whether their model can work in the Philippines and the wider region before they commit serious capital. That includes local entity design, partner selection, commercial terms, governance, and the regulatory path.

The aim is simple: make an early go or no-go decision with enough local evidence to avoid an expensive mistake.

Regulatory strategy for financial services and digital assets
#

Regulation is part of the business model in financial services. I work with leadership teams on licensing strategy, regulator engagement, regulated-product design, and the commercial implications of local rules.

This work is grounded in the reality that a technically sound product can still fail if it does not fit the regulator’s risk language, local market structure, or operating capacity.

Venture building and fintech scale
#

I help established institutions and venture teams build independent ventures with real P&L accountability. The work covers governance, entity structure, talent, incentives, product-market fit, and the operating model required to grow without losing control.

At UBX, we built a leading embedded-finance platform in the Philippines from the ground up, grew it to more than 100 billion in transaction value, and captured 22% market share in its category.

Corporate innovation and venture capital
#

I work with leaders who want more than an innovation lab or a slide deck. Together, we define an investment thesis, decision rights, incentives, portfolio discipline, and exit logic before capital goes out the door.

The standard is measurable value: new revenue, a strategic asset, returned capital, or a clearer decision to stop.

What Makes My Work Different
#

I have sat in the CEO seat
#

I have led operating businesses, not merely advised them. As President and CEO of UBX, the fintech and corporate venture capital arm of UnionBank of the Philippines, I was responsible for building the organization, its ventures, and its results.

That changes the conversation. We focus on decisions that hold up when there is a budget, a regulator, a board, and a team accountable for delivery.

I work from local ground truth
#

Southeast Asia is not one market, and the Philippines cannot be understood through a generic regional deck. Market structure, regulation, ownership rules, conglomerate relationships, and customer behavior all shape what is possible.

I stress-test assumptions against the people and institutions that determine whether a deal or product can move.

I start with commercial viability
#

A license, partnership, or signed memorandum is not success on its own. The question is whether the resulting business has a viable P&L, defensible governance, enough control over its economics, and a credible path to scale or exit.

This is where my approach differs from an international law firm focused only on compliance, a broker focused only on closing, or a strategy consultancy focused on recommendations.

I bring capital-allocation discipline
#

Corporate innovation often becomes expensive theater when nobody defines the return profile, decision rights, or exit conditions. I use the same discipline applied to investment and operating decisions: clear ownership, hard milestones, and an honest view of risk.

At UnionBank’s corporate venture capital effort, the portfolio achieved 3.0x TVPI and returned principal through 1.05x DPI in under four years.

I do not sell generic advice
#

I take on work where I have conviction in the venture, technology, or thesis. I do not work on a success-fee-only basis, and I do not endorse a remote-control approach to Southeast Asia without credible local execution.

If the answer is no, it is better to learn that early.

Who I Work With
#

  • Boards and CEOs of financial institutions building new digital businesses
  • Global fintech, technology, and energy companies entering the Philippines or Southeast Asia
  • Corporate venture teams and innovation leaders responsible for investment and new-venture outcomes
  • Venture capital and private equity investors evaluating local opportunities, acquisitions, or operating partners
  • Venture studios and founders building regulated, capital-intensive, or partnership-dependent businesses
  • Senior executives who need a candid operating view before committing capital, reputation, or management attention

The Operator Behind the Work
#

I am a financial services executive, technology advisor, and former fintech CEO with more than 25 years of experience across banking, insurance, commercial software, and venture building.

My career has taken me through Canada, the United States, and Asia. Most recently, I served as President and CEO of UBX, where we built the Philippines’ leading embedded-finance platform, launched ventures through a venture-studio model, secured strategic investment from SBI Holdings, and introduced PHX, the Philippines’ first central-bank-recognized stablecoin and blockchain-based settlement network.

Before UBX, I held senior operating roles including Chief Operating Officer at Manulife Philippines. Across those roles, the recurring problem has been the same: good ideas fail when leaders treat execution, regulation, capital, and talent as separate workstreams.

I now operate an independent advisory practice. I lead each engagement personally and bring in specialist legal, compliance, regulatory, or technical expertise when the work requires it.

You can find me on LinkedIn, X, and GitHub.

How Engagements Work
#

Start with the facts
#

The first conversation is a reality check, not a sales call. I need the actual business model, leadership context, financial position, product materials, and the specific decision in front of you.

For a market-entry or transaction discussion, that often means reviewing the deck, financials, data room, customer profile, and the team responsible for delivery.

Define the decision and the work
#

Once the facts are clear, we agree on the decision that needs to be made, the scope required to make it, the people involved, and the output. Engagements may include a feasibility assessment, a market-entry plan, a regulatory strategy, a deal structure, a venture-building plan, or ongoing executive advisory.

The work produces a clear decision, operating architecture, or transaction path. It does not produce a generic framework that someone else must decipher.

Work directly and candidly
#

You work with me directly and through focused working sessions with the accountable executives and operators. I keep updates short and decision-useful: a candid executive memo, a working financial model where needed, and verified evidence that supports the recommendation.

I work through defined advisory engagements or retainers, not success-fee-only arrangements. The objective is a clean handoff: a team, structure, deal, or plan that can operate without permanent dependence on an outside advisor.

Frequently Asked Questions
#

What kinds of companies do you advise?
#

I work primarily with financial institutions, fintechs, technology companies, energy and energy-technology ventures, investors, and corporate venture teams. The strongest fit is a serious business facing a high-consequence decision in Southeast Asia.

Do you work with early-stage startups?
#

Yes, when the business has a credible thesis, committed leadership, and a problem worth solving. I am most useful when regulation, market structure, capital needs, or a strategic partnership will materially affect the company’s path.

Do you only work in the Philippines?
#

The Philippines is my deepest operating market, but my work often involves Southeast Asian expansion and companies based in North America. The engagement starts with the actual market, not a regional label.

Do you offer success-fee-only advisory?
#

No. I work through a defined project scope or retainer, because strong advice requires real involvement before a transaction closes. The engagement should put responsibility and incentives on both sides.

What should I prepare before contacting you?
#

Bring the decision you need to make, your current materials, the relevant financial context, and the accountable decision-makers. For market entry or investment work, a corporate overview, deck, data room, and current customer or portfolio information make the first discussion far more useful.
