> **Draft for review.** This is the intended usage policy for this site. It should be reviewed by counsel before publication.

## Use it. Share it. Credit it.

The point of this site is to put useful ideas into circulation. Quote the work. Send it to a colleague. Share a link. Paste a page into an AI assistant and ask better questions.

I ask for two things: give clear credit to the original source, and do not misrepresent my work as yours.

## You are welcome to

* Link to any page on this site.
* Quote excerpts or share complete pages, in a newsletter, presentation, social post, internal memo, or conversation.
* Copy a page into an AI assistant, internal knowledge base, retrieval system, or agent workflow.
* Build on the ideas in your own work, whether for personal, educational, or commercial use.

The Copy Page feature on many of the pages is here to make this easier.

## How to attribute the work

When you quote, share, or reuse content from this site, please include:

* John Januszczak as the author.
* A link to the original page, where practical.
* A clear note when you have edited, condensed, or adapted the material.

A simple attribution is enough:

> Adapted from [John Januszczak](https://januszczak.org/), with a link to the original article.

## Please do not

* Remove the author’s name or present this work as your own.
* Suggest that I endorse you, your company, or your use of the material when I have not said so.
* Bulk-scrape the site to create a competing content database, sell access to a collection of this work, or train or fine-tune a general-purpose AI model on it without permission.

Those are different uses from sharing ideas and having a productive conversation with an AI assistant.

## Third-party material

Some pages quote, link to, or refer to third-party work. This policy covers only the original material published on this site. Respect the rights and terms that apply to everyone else’s work.

## Questions or permissions

If you are unsure about a use, or want to discuss a larger partnership, syndication, or licensing arrangement, [get in touch]({{< relref "contact" >}}). I am usually more interested in a good conversation than a legal argument.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may change as the site and the tools we use change. The date at the top of this page identifies the current version.
