## Content-type and taxonomy rationale

Tags use existing governed tags: venture-building, go-to-market, ecosystem-design, organizational-design, and systems-thinking.

## Research basis and citations

Public research only. ON24's 2025 Webinar Benchmarks article validates the 57% registration-to-attendance benchmark and the material role of on-demand viewing. LinkedIn's B2B Institute article supports the point that events can extend reach, generate content, and improve customer access beyond the event itself. The Tourism Management regional-conference study supports education and networking as attendee motivations connected to satisfaction, word of mouth, and return intent. The 30 to 35% in-person no-show rate and growth from fewer than 20 to more than 110 attendees are treated as author operating data, not universal benchmarks.
