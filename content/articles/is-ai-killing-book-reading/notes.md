# Editorial Notes

## Brief and intended reader

Issue #108 asks whether AI is reducing attention and book reading. The audience is executives and operators adopting AI who need a practical position, not a culture-war answer.

## Content-type and taxonomy rationale

Article, Technology. The post examines AI as an enabling technology and its effect on executive judgment. Tags use the existing canonical set: artificial-intelligence, productivity, systems-thinking, mental-models, and knowledge-management.

## Research basis and citations

- BLS American Time Use Survey, 2024 Results: age-specific reading and leisure-computer-use data.
- Pew Research Center, October 2025 book-reading survey: 75% of U.S. adults reported reading at least part of one book in the prior year.
- CHI 2025 study of 319 knowledge workers: reported cognitive-effort shifts with GenAI, not causal proof.
- Bonifacci et al. meta-analysis: relationship between mind wandering and reading comprehension.
