# Editorial Notes

## Brief and intended reader

Issue #170 asked for a long-form strategy essay for allocators, venture investors, family-office principals, CIOs, and capital allocators who already understand multiples, DCF, and capital allocation. The working question: what should investors buy when capital learns?

## Content-type and taxonomy rationale

Content type: Article.

Category: Technology. The piece treats AI as an enabling force and investment framework rather than as a fund product, fintech mechanism, or personal essay.

Tags: artificial-intelligence, capital-allocation, venture-capital, asset-management, platform-economics, systems-thinking. All are approved canonical tags and match the article's durable themes.
