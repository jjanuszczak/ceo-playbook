## Featured image candidates and selected asset

- Candidate 1, selected: [A view of the server room at The National Archives](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:A_view_of_the_server_room_at_The_National_Archives.jpg). Original file: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e0/A_view_of_the_server_room_at_The_National_Archives.jpg. The National Archives (UK), CC BY 3.0. Selected because the archive setting directly supports the article’s argument about retained institutional memory and the image is landscape, crop-compatible, and free of visible text or watermark.
- Candidate 2: [Wikimedia Servers-0051 17](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wikimedia_Servers-0051_17.jpg). Helpameout, CC BY-SA 3.0. Strong server-room scale, but its framing emphasizes storage infrastructure more than archives and institutional memory.
- Candidate 3: [Wikimedia Foundation Servers 2015-63](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wikimedia_Foundation_Servers_2015-63.jpg). Victor Grigas/Wikimedia Foundation, CC BY-SA 3.0. Clean landscape composition, but less distinctive editorially than the National Archives photograph.
